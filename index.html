Clear answers

Useful before you get started.

What is DocNote and how does it work?

DocNote is an AI-powered medical scribe and clinical documentation assistant for doctors, surgeons and hospitals. It records a consultation securely, transcribes it in real time, generates a structured report, and lets the physician review and validate it before sending it to the electronic health record.

What documentation can DocNote generate?

DocNote can generate consultation notes, SOAP notes, discharge letters, operative reports, progress notes, ward-round notes and emergency department notes.

Which EHR systems does DocNote integrate with?

DocNote integrates directly with Mediway and SOKLE. You can also use your own Word report templates, export to PDF, or copy and paste into another system.

Which specialties and languages does DocNote support?

DocNote supports more than 50 specialties and works in French, German, Italian, English and more than 20 additional languages, including regional terminology and code-switching.

How does DocNote handle healthcare data?

DocNote is built and hosted in Switzerland. The site describes end-to-end encryption, role-based access control, audit logging, Swiss hosting and automatic deletion of data after processing.
